The package was created with OpenAI's built-in image-generation tool. The selected direction is **G — RVI Trace Monogram**, with **Option 2 — Midnight Navy** as the final color treatment.

The prompts below are the final reproducible production prompts for each independently generated asset. Resized icons, favicons, header lockups, and monochrome marks are deterministic derivatives of the generated masters and are documented separately.

## Deterministic derivatives

- The 1024, 512, 256, 128, 64, 32, and 16-pixel application icons are Lanczos-quality PNG reductions of the selected icon master.
- The 512, 192, 180, 48, 32, and 16-pixel favicon files are PNG reductions of the transparent Midnight Navy mark.
- The website header lockups are 320-pixel-wide reductions of the light- and dark-interface horizontal logo masters.
- The solid white and near-black monochrome marks use the alpha silhouette of the transparent Midnight Navy mark, thresholded to remove glow and recolored to `#FFFFFF` and `#050C18` respectively.
- The final GitHub image was normalized to exactly 1280×640, and the final hideouts.io Open Graph image was normalized to exactly 1200×630.
